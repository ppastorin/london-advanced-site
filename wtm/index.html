The 90-day pilot

One property. Four phases. A rollout decision.

The point is not to “run a pilot”. It is to test a commercial hypothesis: will enough exposed guests use the service, act on a recommendation and value the hotel for providing it?

Days 1–14Instrument

Define the guest segment and placements; configure the property start point, co-branding, editorial rules, event taxonomy and measurement plan. Produce QR, reception and digital assets.

Days 15–30Launch

Brief front-of-house staff, go live across two or three controlled touchpoints and establish the first two weeks of baseline engagement.

Days 31–75Optimise

Review the funnel every two weeks. Test one variable at a time—placement, call to action or recommendation framing—without quietly changing the success definition.

Days 76–90Decide

Deliver the data, guest and staff feedback, operational lessons, production scope and an explicit recommendation: stop, iterate or scale.
